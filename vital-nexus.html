Safe codewords — one per kind of danger

Where to use them: type any phrase below into the "quick note" box on Home (it always just looks like a note) or into the Companion chat, or say the phrase out loud during a real phone call to a contact who knows it. Tap a card below to try it and see exactly what happens.
General unease
"Did you feed the cat today?"

Silently alerts your whole circle with your live location — no sound, no visible change on your screen.

Forced to make a call
"Is Mom's soup ready yet?"

Said naturally mid-call. Your contact is primed to hear it as a signal, keep the conversation normal, and quietly alert others for you.

Weapon or immediate danger
"Can you grab my red umbrella?"

Skips the circle entirely — goes straight to emergency responders with your location. Highest priority, no confirmation step.

Being followed
"What time's the dentist tomorrow?"

Shares continuous live location and quietly calls your nearest trusted contact to come find you.

Unsafe ride or taxi
"What's the wifi password there?"

Shares your live trip location with your circle continuously until you check in safe — no alarm shown to the driver.

Unsafe at home
"Has the plumber come by yet?"

Silently alerts your circle and nearest contact with your home address and live status — treated as high priority.

What this prototype stores

Everything you've entered — your name, contacts, medical profile, reminders — stays only in this browser tab's memory. Nothing is sent to a server, nothing is saved once you close the tab, and nothing is shared with anyone unless you explicitly tap a call or share-location button yourself.

Clear everything

Wipes your added contacts, medical entries, reminders, and activity history from this session. Your name and sign-in stay as is.

A welcome that changes every time

Every sign-in shows a different opening note and a fresh three-slide walkthrough of what the app does before you land inside — never the exact same words twice.

A companion that doesn't sound scripted

Guidance for the same condition is worded a little differently each time you ask, and it can also just meditate with you, sit with loneliness, or have a plain conversation — not only emergencies.

Contacts you actually control

Add a real name and real phone number and the call button dials it for real — plus your own local emergency number, entered by you, not assumed for you.

What's genuinely real in this preview: contacts you add use real tel: links to the number you typed, the live GPS card uses your device's actual location with your permission, reminders use real browser notifications, and the companion can speak its replies aloud and listen with your microphone if your browser supports it.

What still needs a native app to be fully true: it can't run in the true background, can't work with zero internet connection at all, and won't auto-dial a number without you pressing call.
